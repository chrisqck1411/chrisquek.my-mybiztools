Data privacy & FAQ

Where do my figures and files go?

Our tools process them inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not keep copies.

How long is working data kept?

Only in this tab's memory, so you can adjust settings without retyping. It clears when you press Clear memory, after 30 minutes of inactivity, or when you close or leave the tab.

Can I check this myself?

Yes. Open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, use a tool, and confirm that no request carries your data. Ads, once enabled, are loaded by Google and are covered by the Privacy Policy.
